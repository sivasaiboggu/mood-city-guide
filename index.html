Most maps optimise for fastest. This one optimises for how you feel. Type a mood, get a three-stop walk — a place to decompress, a place to look around, a place to end on a high.

Offline demo data — no key needed. Distances are straight-line estimates from a seeded list of 48 real Bengaluru places. Add your own free keys in Settings for live AI wording and real street routing.

      How it works

      1. You describe the feeling

      Free text, not a quiz. “Lonely but adventurous” is a perfectly good input.

      2. The scorer picks three stops

      Places are ranked by tag match, cost and distance. The AI never ranks — it only writes the one-line reason, and only when you add a key.

      3. The walk has an arc

      Stop one calms you down, stop two wakes you up, stop three is the reward. Regenerate gives you the next-best set.

      Mood Map is a demo. Mood matching is tags and vibes, not psychology. Distances are estimates until you add an OpenRouteService key; opening hours, crowds and prices are not live. One city, seeded well, beats ten cities seeded thin.
